## 🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is one of the areas I am most passionate about.

I do not see AI as only a tool that generates code. I see it as a new development layer that can improve:

* Research
* Prototyping
* Automation
* Software testing
* Product design
* Content and asset production
* Decision-making
* Development speed

I believe the new generation of developers should learn how to collaborate with AI without blindly trusting it.

My goal is not to memorize every syntax rule. I want to understand:

* Why the system works
* How its components communicate
* Why a particular architecture was selected
* What technical trade-offs were made
* Where the generated solution might fail
* How the system can be improved and maintained

AI can produce code, designs, documents, and assets. However, understanding the problem, designing the system, evaluating the results, identifying risks, and making the final decisions are still engineering responsibilities.

That is the kind of developer I am working to become.

### 🧩 Modular MicroSaaS Infrastructure

I am designing a reusable infrastructure for building and operating multiple independent MicroSaaS products.

Instead of rebuilding the same systems for every new product, the goal is to create a shared foundation that can support several applications while allowing each product to remain independently developed, deployed, and scaled.

The infrastructure is planned to include reusable systems such as:

* Authentication and authorization
* User, team, and organization management
* Multi-tenant data separation
* Subscription and billing infrastructure
* Usage, quota, and credit tracking
* AI provider and model integrations
* Prompt and workflow management
* Email and notification services
* Logging, analytics, and monitoring
* Feature flags and configuration
* Shared administration tools
* API keys and integration management

The goal is not to create one massive tightly coupled monolith.

Each MicroSaaS product should be able to use the shared infrastructure while keeping its own:

* Business logic
* User experience
* Branding
* Database requirements
* Deployment lifecycle
* Pricing model
* Product roadmap

This project reflects one of my main interests: designing systems that are reusable without becoming unnecessarily complicated.

## 😄 A Small Warning

My usual project-development process looks something like this:

1. Find a simple project idea.
2. Decide to build a small MVP.
3. Start thinking about user roles.
4. Add multi-tenant architecture to the plan.
5. Design an AI integration layer.
6. Create a subscription model.
7. Plan reusable infrastructure for five future products.
8. Think about the company structure.
9. Write a three-year roadmap.
10. Return to building the first feature.

I may have a natural ability to turn a weekend project into an international SaaS ecosystem.

But at least the architecture will be documented.
